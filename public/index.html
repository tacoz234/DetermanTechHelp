Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you service?

I am locally based in Lake Barcroft, VA and provide direct house calls throughout Lake Barcroft, Falls Church, Arlington, Alexandria, Bailey's Crossroads, and Seven Corners. If you live slightly outside these borders in Fairfax County, feel free to reach out.

How does hourly billing work?

All appointments carry a simple 1-hour minimum charge. After the first hour, billing is calculated fairly in 15-minute increments. There are no hidden trip charges or travel surcharges for Lake Barcroft residents.

Do you assist senior citizens with patience and clear notes?

Yes, absolutely. A significant portion of my practice involves patient, friendly walkthroughs for seniors. I provide handwritten or printed cheat sheets, simplify passwords safely, and never rush through explanations.

Can you help buy and assemble parts for custom PCs?

Yes! Whether you need a whisper-quiet office workstation, a flight simulator build, or a gaming rig, I can select fully compatible components within your budget, assemble the machine with proper thermal paste application, and install clean Windows without bloatware.
